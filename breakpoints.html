A hero's raw Attack, Defence and Health aren't the buff your troops get — the game runs each one through a diminishing-returns curve that ends in a hard ceiling set by the hero's rarity. Below is the raw stat where each rarity first hits that ceiling, and the buff it caps out at. Past the number shown, more of that flat stat is worth exactly nothing — it's the same total the Army command panel counts down to, gear included.

Rarity

Every figure comes from a curve fitted to observed heroes. Epic is the only rarity with readings sitting on all three of its ceilings; Legendary and Rare rest on fewer, and the bottom two are extrapolated from the pattern the others set rather than measured.
