Auto-optimize Everything

←

One pass over your whole roster: every hero gets auto-optimized in turn, top of the list first. Gear is exclusive, so the order is the whole point — whoever is higher up gets first pick, and everyone below them builds from what's left.

  1. Every hero in the list is stripped first. Nothing is assigned on the basis of who happened to be wearing it — a piece goes to the highest-priority hero who wants it. Slots you've locked in the builder are left alone at both ends: never stripped, never re-picked.
  2. Then each hero is filled, in order. Each one is scored with its own preset and set bonuses, using exactly the same optimizer the Auto-optimize menu runs for a single hero.
  3. Your own preset defaults are used. If you've saved weights for a preset with Save as default for, those are the weights a hero on that preset is built with — not the built-in ones.
  4. A hero set to Don't optimize ends up with nothing equipped. That's what putting one at the bottom with no preset is for: their gear goes back into the pool for the heroes above them.
  5. Instanced loadouts are never touched. Those are your dungeon sets — they keep their gear, and that gear stays reserved for them, exactly as it would be in a single-hero optimize.

Configuration

Highest priority at the top. Change a hero's preset or set bonuses with the dropdowns, and move them with the arrows. Your changes are saved as you make them, by hero name, so they survive a re-import.
